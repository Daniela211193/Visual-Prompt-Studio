Create an album cover that features the 1980’s synthwave. It should feature a boy and girl that are listening to music with a boombox on a dance floor, with a skyline and sunrise in the background. The main color scheme should be vibrant sunset colors to illustrate the theme of the genre. Rhythm should be illustrated with irregular lines forming musical notes and setting a fun vibe to the album cover. The boy and girl should have shadows to demonstrate their motion in dancing. In the skyline, each building should be in a different shape and color with a polished chrome surface allowing them to complement each other. An illusion should be created by the buildings disappearing into the sunrise.  The boy and girl should be symmetrically balanced, giving them all the attention.   

Genre Choice: Synthwave / Retro 1980s: Focus on glowing neon grids, vibrant sunset color palettes, polished chrome surfaces, and geometric laser lines. 
 
Value:The lightness or darkness an artist uses to set a tone or mood.

Space:The illusion created by the size of an object.  

Unity:Different ideas of the same theme used together to make one design 

Balance:The side of the art piece the artist wants the viewer to focus on

Rhythm:A pattern or repetition of something found in the art piece.
