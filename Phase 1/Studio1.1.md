Assignment:Visual Prompt Studio 1.1

Visual Prompt: Create a sci-fi poster showcasing a human-like creature standing in the middle of an abandoned scientific lab that has no roof with an army surrounding it. There should be a use of a lot of irregular lines to describe the scene as frightening. The use of directional lines that feature other buildings around the lab demonstrating that the creature is tall. The main colors should be white and dark colors to show the intense and mysterious night. There is a skyline smaller than the creature with metallic panels to give the illusion that the creature is tall.  

Vocabulary

Lines: A line connects two different objects together  

Shape: The form of something 

Texture: The imagination a pattern gives us based on previous patterns you have felt before. 

Volume: When an artist makes an art piece look full by using an object that leaves a small opening between each other.  

Contrast: When to opposite things are put together and are well aligned
